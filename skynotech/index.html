Portfolio Highlights

Featured Projects

Exploring technology through practical innovation and real-world problem solving.

Edge Rescue

An intelligent disaster-response technology project that uses drone-based data collection and machine learning to analyze the current situation, estimate the presence of survivors, and provide predictive insights to help rescue teams assess whether entering the affected area is safe.

Autonomous Refrigerator Management

An intelligent refrigerator-management project designed to monitor food-related conditions and automatically adjust the refrigerator temperature based on detected food smell and related environmental conditions.

IoT-Based machine Monitoring Car

An IoT-enabled industrial monitoring system designed to continuously monitor industrial environments and machines. RFID is used to identify and track specific machines, while sensor data such as temperature and humidity is analyzed along with historical data using machine learning to identify potential machine-failure conditions.

Landslide Monitoring System

An IoT and machine-learning based landslide prediction system that collects current environmental sensor data along with historical data and processes the information using machine learning to predict whether a landslide may occur in a monitored area.

RFID-Based Attendance System

An RFID-based attendance system where users can mark their entry attendance by placing their RFID card on the reader and mark their exit attendance by scanning the card again.

Smart Energy Assistant

An intelligent energy-monitoring solution designed to monitor electricity consumption, analyze usage patterns and provide practical insights to help users understand and improve energy efficiency.
